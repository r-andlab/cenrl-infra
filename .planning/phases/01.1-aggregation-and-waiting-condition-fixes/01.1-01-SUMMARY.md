# Phase 01.1 Plan 01: Aggregator VP Snapshot Fix and Funneler Capabilities Summary

register_targets() snapshots expected VPs at scheduling time to prevent race condition where VPs complete evaluation between scheduling and first result; call_go_api dispatches GET via requests.get; remove_vantage_points() sends POST /remove-vantage-points with unstarted_work warning logging.

## Task Results
